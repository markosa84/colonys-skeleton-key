# A 7-plate chest the tool reported as "no lock detected" (2026-08-07 21:53, three F8 presses, three
# byte-identical reads; this is the third, captures/no-lock-20260807-215336-589.png). Nothing about
# the environment was wrong: borderless 3840x2160, DPI per-monitor-v2, awt scale 1.00, viewport
# 3840x2160+0+0, gamma probe ink 91 - on-family, so Tone mapped the frame back to the calibration.
# It is neither the wrong-viewport dead end nor HDR.
#
# WHAT IT PINS. Six of the seven rows walked a clean 6/6. The BACK plate walked 3/6, so the 7-plate
# fan was rejected; the 5-plate fan (rows 1..5 of the same lattice) then fitted and was CORRECTLY
# rejected by the beyond-check, because the row past its front end is a real plate. detectPlateCount
# returned -1 and the player was told there was no lock.
#
# The cause is in how a plate's steel was measured, and this frame is the one that exposes it. The
# strip a row is sampled in is 2 * ROW_MAX_DX + 1 px wide, because a row CAN be that wide - but this
# back plate sits at +3, so it has slid until its body ends about 170px right of its pin, and the
# remaining ~175px of strip reads a median of 8: the black room behind the lock. A quarter of the
# strip off the plate, plus the fifth of it that is the six holes, outvoted the steel and the median
# came back 162 where the steel between the holes reads 210-255. The tracing gate collapsed with it,
# three of that row's six holes traced as fragments (34/46 and 51/67 px) or landed 8 px under the
# 150 px area floor, and the row walked 3/6. Fixed by measuring the BRIGHT POPULATION of the strip
# instead of its median (LatticeReader.brightMedian), which is also what re-referenced HOLE_DARK.
#
# The room is what makes it visible: the 7p-* fixtures have their back plate at +3 too, but there the
# area past the plate's edge is not pitch black, so the median survived it.
#
# HOW THE LABELS WERE ESTABLISHED. Not from the reader being repaired. Sampling the RAW luminance
# along each row's deskewed line - no blob detection, no metal estimate, no walk - and cutting at the
# midpoint of that line's own min and max, every row shows six dips on the hole lattice at its own
# spacing, and offset = (dips left of the pin) - 3 gives the states below:
#
#   row 0  -263 -221 -178 -135  -91  -45                     six left,  spacing ~44  ->  +3
#   row 1  -222 -180 -136  -91  -46  +47                     five left, spacing ~44  ->  +2
#   row 2  -269 -227 -182 -139  -95  -49                     six left,  spacing ~44  ->  +3
#   row 3   +45  +94 +143 +192 +244 +291                     six right, spacing ~49  ->  -3
#   row 4   -96  -49  +46  +94 +143 +195                     two left,  spacing ~48  ->  -1
#   row 5   +44  +95 +144 +195 +247 +293                     six right, spacing ~50  ->  -3
#   row 6   +46  +95 +145 +197 +249 +295                     six right, spacing ~50  ->  -3
#
# That census also finds isolated dips OFF the lattice (row 0 at +235, row 3 at -247, row 6 at -249
# and -123). They are not holes: none of them sits a hole spacing from a hole, and every one of them
# lies past the end of its plate. They are the black room the sampling strip runs into - i.e. the
# very thing this frame is here to pin - and they are why the crude census is quoted with its
# lattice reading rather than as a bare count.
